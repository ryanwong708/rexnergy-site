Hospitality Support Services

Helping You Deliver Exceptional Guest Experiences

Running a successful homestay or hotel involves much more than maintaining the property. Every guest expects a clean room, a smooth check-in experience and prompt support throughout their stay.

Rexnergy provides operational support services that help accommodation operators improve efficiency, reduce workload and maintain consistent service standards. Whether you manage a single homestay or multiple accommodation units, we're here to help keep your operations running smoothly.

Talk To Our Team
🏨

Homestay & Hotel Housekeeping

Professional housekeeping services designed to prepare every room for the next guest. Our housekeeping services include:

  • Room turnover cleaning
  • Bed making
  • Bathroom cleaning
  • Kitchen cleaning (where applicable)
  • Waste disposal
  • Restocking guest amenities (customer-supplied)
  • Final room readiness inspection
🧺

Commercial Linen Laundry

Fresh, clean linen is essential for creating a comfortable guest experience. Our laundry service includes:

  • Bed sheets
  • Pillowcases
  • Duvet covers
  • Towels
  • Bath mats
  • Kitchen towels (where applicable)

Collection and delivery services can be arranged based on your operational schedule.

📅

Reservation Management

  • Platforms:
  • Airbnb
  • Booking.com
  • Agoda
  • Direct bookings
  • Other supported platforms
  • Services include:
  • Booking management
  • Calendar updates
  • Guest communication
  • Reservation coordination

Guest Verification

Protect your property with a structured guest verification process. Our support includes:

  • Identity verification
  • Collection of required guest information
  • Compliance with your house rules
  • Secure record management

Deposit Collection & Refund Management

Simplify your guest deposit process with structured handling and clear communication. Services include:

  • Deposit collection coordination
  • Deposit tracking
  • Refund processing
  • Documentation support
  • Guest communication

Guest Check-in & Check-out Coordination

Deliver a smoother arrival and departure experience for every guest. Support services include:

  • Guest arrival coordination
  • Self check-in guidance
  • Check-out instructions
  • Late arrival support
  • Basic guest assistance

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you support both homestays and hotels?
Yes. We support Airbnb hosts, serviced apartments, boutique hotels, budget hotels and other accommodation providers.
Can I engage only one service?
Yes. You may engage individual services such as housekeeping or reservation management, or combine multiple services into a customised support package.
Do you provide daily housekeeping?
Yes. Housekeeping schedules can be arranged based on your occupancy and operational requirements.
Do you provide collection and delivery for linen laundry?
Yes. Collection and delivery can be scheduled to match your room turnover requirements.
Which booking platforms do you support?
We can assist with reservations from major OTA platforms such as Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda and direct bookings, subject to your preferred workflow.
Can you manage multiple properties?
Yes. Our services are designed to support operators managing multiple homestays, serviced apartments or hotels across different locations.
Can your services be customised?
Absolutely. Every accommodation business operates differently, so we'll tailor a support solution that fits your property size, occupancy and operational needs.
Which areas do you serve?
We primarily support accommodation operators in Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Old Klang Road and surrounding Klang Valley areas.
