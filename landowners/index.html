Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help even if I don't have a final development plan?
Yes. Many clients come to us with an idea rather than a complete plan. We can discuss suitable options based on your land and objectives.
Do I need electricity before installing a cabin?
Not necessarily. We can recommend off-grid solar and battery solutions where utility power is unavailable.
Can the project be completed in stages?
Yes. Many landowners begin with a cabin or basic infrastructure and expand over time.
Can you customise cabin designs?
Yes. Cabin layouts and features can be tailored to your intended use and site requirements.
Do you provide ongoing maintenance?
Yes. We continue to support completed projects with maintenance, engineering services, security upgrades and energy solutions.
Which areas do you serve?
We primarily serve Kuala Lumpur, Selangor and surrounding regions. For projects outside these areas, please contact us to discuss your requirements.
