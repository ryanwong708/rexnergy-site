Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can cabins be customised?
Yes. Cabin layouts, sizes, finishes and fittings can be customised to suit your intended use.
How long does fabrication take?
The timeline depends on the cabin size, level of customisation and current production schedule. We'll provide an estimated completion timeframe during the quotation stage.
Do you provide transportation and installation?
Yes. We can coordinate transportation and installation for completed cabins, subject to site accessibility.
Can solar systems be installed on cabins?
Yes. Our cabins can be designed to accommodate rooftop solar panels and off-grid battery systems where suitable.
Can cabins include plumbing and electrical systems?
Yes. We can integrate plumbing, electrical wiring, lighting, air conditioning and other essential services based on your requirements.
Are cabins suitable for commercial use?
Yes. Our modular solutions are suitable for businesses, showrooms, kiosks, offices and many other commercial applications.
Can you help me choose the right cabin?
Absolutely. We'll recommend a solution based on your intended use, available land and budget.
