Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I service my air conditioner?
For residential properties, we generally recommend servicing every 3–6 months, depending on usage. Hotels, homestays and commercial properties with higher usage may benefit from more frequent servicing as part of a preventive maintenance programme.
When is chemical cleaning recommended?
Chemical cleaning is typically recommended when an air conditioner is no longer cooling effectively, has significant dirt build-up, emits unpleasant odours or experiences water leakage. Our technicians will assess whether a normal service or chemical cleaning is the most appropriate solution.
Do you install new air conditioning systems?
Yes. We provide installation services for new air conditioning systems and can recommend suitable capacities based on your room size and usage.
Do you provide emergency repair services?
Yes. We strive to respond promptly to urgent engineering maintenance issues, subject to technician availability.
Can you maintain multiple properties?
Yes. We support homeowners with multiple residences, accommodation operators and businesses managing properties across different locations.
Do you provide scheduled maintenance contracts?
Yes. We offer customised preventive maintenance programmes for residential, commercial and hospitality properties.
Do you supply spare parts?
Yes. Where replacement parts are required, we will recommend suitable components and provide transparent pricing before carrying out any repair work.
Which areas do you serve?
We primarily serve Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Old Klang Road and surrounding areas within the Klang Valley.
