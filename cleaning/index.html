Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to provide cleaning equipment?
We prefer to use your cleaning equipment if available; our team can also bring the necessary cleaning equipment and supplies. If you have preferred products, we're happy to use them upon request.
How long does a cleaning session take?
The duration depends on the property size and cleaning scope. We'll provide an estimated timeframe when confirming your booking.
Can I book recurring cleaning services?
Yes. Weekly, fortnightly and monthly cleaning schedules are available.
What's the difference between regular and deep cleaning?
Regular cleaning focuses on maintaining cleanliness, while deep cleaning targets accumulated dirt, grease and hard-to-reach areas that aren't usually covered during routine cleaning.
Do you provide housekeeping for Airbnb and hotels?
Yes. We support homestays, serviced apartments, boutique hotels and other accommodation providers with professional housekeeping services.
Do you provide post-renovation cleaning?
Yes. Our team is equipped to clean construction dust, paint residue and renovation debris before handover or occupancy.
Which areas do you serve?
We primarily serve Kuala Lumpur, Petaling Jaya, Old Klang Road and surrounding areas within the Klang Valley.
