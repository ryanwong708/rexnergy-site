Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which CCTV system is suitable for my property?
The most suitable system depends on your property size, monitoring requirements and budget. We'll recommend an appropriate solution after understanding your needs.
Can I view my CCTV remotely?
Yes. Most modern systems support secure remote viewing through a smartphone or computer.
How long can CCTV recordings be stored?
Recording duration depends on the hard drive capacity, number of cameras and recording settings. We can recommend storage options based on your requirements.
Which type of autogate is best?
The ideal system depends on your gate design and available space. We install and support swing, folding and sliding autogate systems.
Can access control systems support multiple users?
Yes. Depending on the system selected, multiple users can be registered using fingerprints, RFID cards, PIN codes or a combination of authentication methods.
Do you provide maintenance services?
Yes. We provide preventive maintenance, repairs, troubleshooting and system upgrades.
Do you provide a warranty?
Yes. Warranty coverage varies depending on the equipment selected. We'll explain the applicable warranty before installation.
